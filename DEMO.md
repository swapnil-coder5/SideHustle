# Tonight's demo

## A 90-second story

**0–12 seconds — the problem**

“A client says ‘just one more thing.’ Your fixed-fee website quietly becomes a shop, more pages, and another revision. ScopeShield helps make the extra work visible before you do it.”

Show the workspace. Keep the browser at 100% zoom on a desktop.

**12–28 seconds — both sides**

Select **The ‘tiny’ website update** → **Load example**. Point at the explicit exclusions and used revision rounds, then the client's casual request.

“The agreement and the new request are the whole input. No inbox connection. No client document uploaded to cloud AI.”

**28–45 seconds — real AI**

Show **Local AI settings** with `qwen2.5:1.5b` selected. Run **Find the scope creep**.

Inference may be slow on CPU. Record the actual run ahead of time and cut the waiting time honestly; label the cut “local inference, waiting time shortened.” Do not use the sample report and call it a live AI result.

**45–65 seconds — evidence**

“These quotes come from both documents. The app checks that they exist. Unverifiable evidence is marked for review and kept out of the proposed fee.”

Show an extra and an included request. Be candid about any model mistake. A verified quote does not prove correct interpretation.

**65–80 seconds — control**

Enter your own estimates: for the website example, try 12 hours for the shop, 6 for pages, and 3 for the revision. These are illustrative assumptions. Live reports start at zero and require positive hours before proposal export. Deselect an extra and switch reply tones. Show that the total updates. Download a change order. No client message is sent.

**80–90 seconds — why open, why pay**

“The open model runs on hardware I control. After setup, this can work offline, with no per-review API fee. I'd test a $29 packaged edition with freelance designers who need help protecting their project margins.”
