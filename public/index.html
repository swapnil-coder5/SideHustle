BOUNDARIES, WITHOUT THE DRAMA.

From “quick change”
to clear change order.

  1. Give it both sides.

    Paste the agreement and the request. Context helps: revision rounds used, exclusions, and agreed deliverables.

  2. Local AI compares them.

    The model separates included work, potential extras, and ambiguity. Source quotes are checked against your actual documents.

  3. You make the call.

    Review the evidence, adjust the hours, and choose the items to propose. You set the hours; the AI classifies scope.

  4. Send a better conversation.

    Copy a reply, download a Markdown change order, or print a PDF. Nothing is sent automatically.

Why open? Your confidential agreements stay on hardware you control. Swap the model, inspect the prompt, and keep working offline after setup.
